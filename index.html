Русский

  • Все записи (кормления, сцеживание, вес, имена детей) хранятся только на вашем телефоне. У приложения нет своего сервера; разработчик не получает, не собирает и не передаёт ваши данные третьим лицам.
  • Нет рекламы, аналитики и отслеживания.

  • Файлы, которые вы сами сохраняете или отправляете (резервная копия, CSV, PDF), попадают только туда, куда вы их отправили.

English

  • Feeding Diary (developer: Hlushchenko Apps) helps you log feeds, pumping and your baby's weight.
  • All entries (feeds, pumping, weight, children's names) are stored only on your phone. The app has no server; the developer does not receive, collect or share your data with third parties.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • If Google backup is enabled on your phone, Android stores a copy of the app's data in your Google account. This is managed by Google and can be turned off in phone settings.
  • Voice input uses Google's system speech recognition: the phrase is processed by Google and only the text is returned to the app.
  • Files you save or send yourself (backup, CSV, PDF) go only where you send them.
  • Voluntary “Support” payments are processed by Google Play; the app never sees payment details.
  • To delete all data, uninstall the app or clear its data in phone settings.
  • The app is not a medical device and does not replace medical advice.
  • Contact: stroggpar@gmail.com
